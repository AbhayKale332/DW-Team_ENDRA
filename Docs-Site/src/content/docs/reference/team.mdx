## Data

- **GAMUS**: geometry-aware multi-modal semantic segmentation benchmark (nDSM + classes).
- **SynRS3D**: synthetic remote-sensing 3D dataset.
- **IEEE GRSS Data Fusion Contest 2023 (Track 2)** and **2019 (US3D)**.
- **IARPA Multi-View Stereo 3D Mapping** benchmark: imagery courtesy of DigitalGlobe, approved for public release.
- **NEON**: National Ecological Observatory Network airborne LiDAR.
- **Copernicus DEM**: produced using Copernicus WorldDEM-30 © DLR e.V. 2010–2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014–2018, provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA; all rights reserved.
- **SRTM / NASADEM** (NASA), **Terrarium tiles** (AWS Open Data), **OpenStreetMap** contributors (ODbL).
- Screenshots marked © Google Earth are used for illustration only.
