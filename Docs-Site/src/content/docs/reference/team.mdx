## Data

- **[GAMUS](https://huggingface.co/datasets/earthflow/GAMUS)**: geometry-aware multi-modal semantic segmentation benchmark (nDSM + classes).
- **[SynRS3D](https://huggingface.co/datasets/JTRNEO/SynRS3D)**: synthetic remote-sensing 3D dataset.
- **IEEE GRSS Data Fusion Contest [2023 (Track 2)](https://ieee-dataport.org/competitions/2023-ieee-grss-data-fusion-contest-large-scale-fine-grained-building-classification)** and **[2019 (US3D)](https://ieee-dataport.org/open-access/data-fusion-contest-2019-dfc2019)**.
- **[IARPA Multi-View Stereo 3D Mapping](https://spacenet.ai/iarpa-multi-view-stereo-3d-mapping/)** benchmark: imagery courtesy of DigitalGlobe, approved for public release.
- **[NEON AOP](https://data.neonscience.org/data-products/DP3.30015.001)**: National Ecological Observatory Network airborne LiDAR (canopy height model).
- **Copernicus DEM**: produced using Copernicus WorldDEM-30 © DLR e.V. 2010–2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014–2018, provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA; all rights reserved.
- **SRTM / NASADEM** (NASA), **Terrarium tiles** (AWS Open Data), **OpenStreetMap** contributors (ODbL).
- **Cartosat-2S / 2E** sample products: [NRSC Bhoonidhi](https://bhoonidhi.nrsc.gov.in/bhoonidhi/help/sampleProducts.html).
- Screenshots marked © Google Earth are used for illustration only.

Packed training stores and the trained model are listed under [Download the data](/training/datasets/#download-the-data).
