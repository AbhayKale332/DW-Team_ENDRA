## Research papers

- [DepthWizard manuscript](/reference/paper/) — project method and results; in preparation.
- [DINOv3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.10104) — satellite-pretrained encoder.
- [Vision Transformers for Dense Prediction (DPT)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.13413) — decoder design.
- [AdaBins](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.14141) — adaptive depth bins.
- [Depth Anything V2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09414) — backbone ablation.
- [GAMUS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14914) · [SynRS3D](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.18151) — benchmark and synthetic training data.
- [IM2HEIGHT](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.10249) · [IM2ELEVATION](https://doi.org/10.3390/rs12172719) — single-view height estimation.
