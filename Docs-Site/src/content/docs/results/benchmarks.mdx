## Same-protocol test comparison

To remove validation-set differences, v3, v4-modal and the DAv2 ablation were scored with **one protocol on the same held-out tiles** (`eval_test.py --compare`).

The dedicated [Depth Anything V2 vs DINOv3 comparison](/results/encoder-comparison/) explains the model choice, landscape differences and limits of this comparison, including DAv2's larger input grid and different training settings.

<Aside type="note">On GAMUS **validation**, v3 beats v4-modal (2.61 vs 3.63 m with TTA). On GAMUS **test**, v4-modal beats v3 (3.32 vs 3.57 m). The validation split is 87.5 % urban against 57.6 % in test, and the two runs saw different validation packings. That is why held-out test results are the headline here.</Aside>
