DepthWizard v5 epoch-7 `best.pt` completed inference on 67 Cartosat crops. No scene DSM ground truth is available, so these numbers check coarse DEM agreement and pipeline behavior; they do not measure building or tree height accuracy.

SRTM and NASADEM are historical surface models, and are not independent of each other. Copernicus is the model's elevation anchor, so its low error is not independent accuracy evidence. The outputs passed grid, projection, GSD, and vertical-datum checks.
