The **IARPA Multi-View Stereo 3D Mapping (MVS3DM)** benchmark pairs WorldView-3 imagery of San Fernando, Argentina with airborne LiDAR. DepthWizard v5 was run on a pansharpened true-ortho of one scene (0.3 m, 5.4° off-nadir) and compared with a LiDAR nDSM at the **8 official benchmark sites**. These sites form the `test` split in `tools/pack_mvs3dm.py`, and tiles within a buffer around them are dropped, so they were never trained on.

<Aside type="note" title="Provenance">
The outputs were produced by a v5 checkpoint. The output metadata does not record which one, but v5 training runs that included MVS3DM used only its `train` and `val` splits. The LiDAR ground truth is a morphological DTM subtracted from the despiked LiDAR DSM, because the LAZ points are unclassified.
</Aside>
