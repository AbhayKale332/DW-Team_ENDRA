## Cartosat-2E, georeferenced (absolute DSM)

A 1024 × 1024 px Cartosat-2E crop at 0.6 m (EPSG:32644, village and scrubland), processed by v5 with `absolute` enabled. The nDSM maxes out at 11.8 m and 92.3 % of pixels are below 1 m, as expected for a sparse rural scene. The DSM combines the DEM-anchored terrain with the nDSM, in metres above EGM2008.
