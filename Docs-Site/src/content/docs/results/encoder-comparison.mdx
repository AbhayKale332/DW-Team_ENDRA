DepthWizard retained **DINOv3 ViT-L/16 SAT-493M** as its encoder. In the completed **Depth Anything V2 Base** experiment (`DAV2_V1`), DINOv3's v4-modal configuration had lower overall GAMUS test RMSE with plain inference and TTA, and lower India validation RMSE. Its satellite pretraining also matches the imagery DepthWizard processes.

**The advantage is modest and depends on the inference setting:** GAMUS test TTA RMSE is **3.321 m for DINOv3 versus 3.396 m for DAv2**, a **0.074 m (2.19%)** reduction from the unrounded metrics. DAv2 wins with sliding-window TTA. The choice therefore reflects the deployment target and measured trade-offs, rather than a win on every metric.

## What the experiment compared

The DAv2 variant reused the v4 training and evaluation pipeline, retaining DepthWizard's metric height, segmentation and normal heads. It kept DAv2's **pretrained encoder and DPT neck**, removed its original relative-depth head, and fine-tuned for **height above ground in metres (nDSM)**. Both variants predict metric heights after training; this is a comparison of adapted DepthWizard models.

| Property | Depth Anything V2 Base · DAV2/V1 | DINOv3 · v4-modal |
|---|---|---|
| Encoder | DINOv2 ViT-B/14 | DINOv3 ViT-L/16 SAT-493M |
| Encoder parameters, approximately | 86 M | 303 M |
| Pretraining emphasis | Monocular depth with synthetic labels and real pseudo-labels | Self-supervised satellite imagery |
| DPT neck at the start of training | Pretrained for depth | Randomly initialised |
| Patch / input tile | 14 px / 518 × 518 px | 16 px / 512 × 512 px |
| Decoder channels | 128 | 256 |
| Completed epochs | 40 | 30 |
| Unfrozen encoder blocks | All 12 | Top 16 of 24 |
| Layer-wise LR decay | 0.85 | 0.90 |
| Stratum weighting β / clip | 0.5 / 5.0 | 0.7 / 8.0 |

Architecture details come from `DAV2_V1/README.md` and `models/dav2.py`; the run settings above come from the completed metrics files. The upstream [DAv2 model card](https://huggingface.co/depth-anything/Depth-Anything-V2-Base-hf) describes its DINOv2/DPT architecture and depth training, while Meta's [DINOv3 model card](https://huggingface.co/facebook/dinov3-vitl16-pretrain-sat493m) records SAT-493M's 493 million satellite images.

<Aside type="caution" title="Shared evaluation, different training configurations">
This is a comparison of trained configurations, not an isolated encoder ablation. Encoder size, decoder initialisation, loss weighting, unfreezing, epoch budgets and training data all differ. DINOv3 v4-modal also trained on DFC23; DAv2's completed configuration did not. The shared evaluation report uses the same source splits and inference modes, but DAv2's 518 px grid scores more pixels than DINOv3's 512 px grid. These results support a model choice, but do not establish that pretraining alone caused the difference.
</Aside>

## Why DINOv3 was retained

1. **Better overall accuracy in the principal test modes.** The v4-modal DINOv3 configuration had lower GAMUS test RMSE in plain and TTA inference, lower balanced RMSE, and lower India validation RMSE. This favours DINOv3 when the goal is overall metric height accuracy under these settings.
2. **Pretraining matches the input domain.** SAT-493M uses satellite imagery, whereas DAv2's pretrained neck is specialised for monocular depth. Overhead height estimation depends on cues such as roof structure, shadows and canopy texture. Satellite pretraining is a plausible reason to prefer DINOv3's features for this task; the experiment does not isolate or prove that mechanism.
3. **The test supports the project's canopy and tall-height priorities.** DINOv3 v4-modal had lower forested and tall-pixel TTA RMSE. The later [v5 training choices](/training/findings/#stratum-weighting-as-a-trade-off) continue this encoder while addressing canopy errors through data and loss changes. Those later v5 results are separate from this experiment.

DAv2 remains a credible alternative: its encoder is roughly **3.5× smaller**, its neck starts with depth-specific weights, and it wins on sliding-window RMSE and sparse landscapes. Parameter count alone does not establish a latency or memory advantage in deployment; this comparison did not record a matched inference-speed benchmark.

The tested **DAv2 Base** weights also carry a **CC-BY-NC-4.0** licence, as documented in the [upstream repository](https://github.com/DepthAnything/Depth-Anything-V2#license). This is an additional deployment consideration. DINOv3 uses Meta's [DINOv3 licence](https://huggingface.co/facebook/dinov3-vitl16-pretrain-sat493m#model-description); it should not be described as Apache-2.0. DAv2 Small has a different licence and was not the model tested here.
